What we do

  • Clean up coastlines

    Hands-on cleanups along San Luis Obispo's coastline and trails.

  • Foster forests

    Forest restoration days that help SLO's hills and open spaces grow back greener.

  • Create communities

    Info sessions, concerts and cleanups that turn people who care into people who show up.

Over 77 pounds, every time we go out

From the P hike to downtown streets, our cleanups average more than 77 lbs of trash. Our four biggest alone pulled 312.9 lbs out of San Luis Obispo.

Built by students, funded by people who care

The Green Shovel Project started with Cal Poly student Koby Kaplan and his peers, who saw the need for direct action and decided to take it. Today we host events every month and run 100% on donations, so every contribution goes straight into the work.

How a season runs

  1. Monthly cleanupsCoastline and trail cleanups around San Luis Obispo.
  2. Info sessionsUnderstand your impact on the environment and what you can do about it.
  3. Closing ceremonyWe celebrate what everyone pulled off together.
